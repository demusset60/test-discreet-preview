How it works FAQ

Refund policy

Unopened kits in their original sealed packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Cooling-off period

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you have 14 days from delivery to cancel your order for any reason. Because test kits are sealed for hygiene and safety, this right ends once a kit's packaging is opened.

Opened kits

Opened test kits cannot be returned or refunded for hygiene and safety reasons. This does not affect your rights if a kit is faulty.

Faulty or invalid tests

If a kit arrives damaged, is past its expiry date, or returns an invalid result (no line at C), we replace it free of charge. Email a photo of the cassette or packaging to help@testdiscreet.co.uk with your order number.

How to return

Email help@testdiscreet.co.uk with your order number and we will send return instructions. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the return. Banks can take a few extra days to show the credit.
