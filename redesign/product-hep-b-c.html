Why test
  • Both viruses can sit symptomless for years while quietly damaging the liver.
  • Hepatitis C is now curable in over 95% of cases with a short course of tablets.
  • Hepatitis B is manageable with treatment once you know you have it.
What’s in the box
  • Combo test cassette in sealed foil pouch
  • Buffer solution
  • 2 sterile lancets
  • Capillary pipette
  • Alcohol pad and plaster
  • Instructions for use
How to use it
  1. Wash your hands in warm water. Clean your fingertip with the alcohol pad.
  2. Press the lancet against the side of your fingertip until it clicks. Squeeze out a drop of blood.
  3. Fill the pipette to the line, dispense into both sample wells, add 2 drops of buffer to each.
  4. Read your result at 15 minutes, not after 20.
When to test
  • Hepatitis B - from 4 weeks after exposure
  • Hepatitis C - from 9 weeks; repeat at 6 months after a known exposure for a conclusive result
Reading your result
  • One line at C - negative
  • Two lines, even faint - positive. Take the result to your GP or a sexual health clinic to confirm it and start treatment
  • No line at C - invalid test. Email us for a free replacement
