How accurate are the tests?

Every kit is a CE-marked in-vitro diagnostic using the colloidal gold immunoassay method, the same technology used for rapid testing in clinics. Performance data for each kit is printed in its instructions for use. Any positive result should be confirmed with a laboratory test through your GP or a sexual health clinic.

When should I test after unprotected sex?

Each infection has a window before it shows on a test.

  • Chlamydia and gonorrhoea - 2 weeks
  • Syphilis - 3 to 6 weeks; conclusive at 12
  • HIV - 3 to 6 weeks; conclusive at 12
  • Hepatitis B - 4 weeks
  • Hepatitis C - 9 weeks

Testing too early can give false reassurance. Each product page states the window for that test.

What happens if I test positive?

Take a photo of the cassette and see your GP or a sexual health clinic. They confirm the result with a laboratory test and start treatment. Chlamydia, gonorrhoea and syphilis usually clear with a single course of antibiotics.

What does the parcel look like?

A plain outer box with a standard shipping label. Nothing on the outside names the product or the shop.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day by free tracked delivery. Most UK orders arrive within 1 to 2 working days.

Can I return a kit?
